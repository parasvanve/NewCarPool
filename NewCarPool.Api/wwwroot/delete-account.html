Delete Your CarPool Account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data

If you no longer wish to use CarPool, you can request permanent deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it. Please read the details below before submitting your request.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to our support team using the button below, or manually email us at kallwik.in@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include the following in your email:

Email Us to Request Deletion

This will open your default email app with a pre-filled request. Please make sure to fill in your registered email in the message before sending.

What Gets Deleted

DataAction
Name, email address, phone numberPermanently deleted
Profile photo delete-account.html / uploaded documents Permanently deleted
Saved locations and ride historyPermanently deleted
Account login credentialsPermanently deleted
Records required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes (if any)Retained only as long as legally required, then deleted

Timeline

We will process your account deletion request within 30 days of receiving it. Once processed, your account and personal data will be permanently removed from our systems and cannot be recovered.
