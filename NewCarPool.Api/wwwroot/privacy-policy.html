Privacy Policy for CarPool

Last updated: August 22, 2026

CarPool ("we", "our", "the app") is a carpooling and ride-sharing application developed by Kallwik Technology. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using CarPool, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Data TypeWhy We Collect It
Name and Email Address To create and manage your account, verify your identity, and enable communication between riders and drivers.
Location (GPS) To show your position on the map, match you with nearby rides, calculate routes, and estimate distance/fare.
Camera / Photos To let you set or update a profile picture, or upload documents required for driver verification.
Phone Number (if provided) To verify your account and allow riders/drivers to coordinate a trip.

We only collect what is necessary to provide carpooling functionality. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, secure, and manage your CarPool account
  • To match riders and drivers and calculate trip routes/fares
  • To show your live location during an active trip for safety and coordination
  • To send you booking confirmations, trip updates, or account-related emails
  • To improve app performance and troubleshoot issues

3. Location Data

CarPool uses your device's GPS location to power core features such as finding nearby rides, navigation, and fare estimation. Location access can be enabled or disabled at any time from your device's Settings, though this will limit or disable ride-matching features. We do not collect location data in the background when the app is not in use.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • With other users: your name, profile photo, and approximate location may be visible to a matched rider/driver to complete a trip.
  • Service providers: we use third-party services (e.g., Google Maps, OpenRouteService) to power maps, routing, and location features.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect user safety.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers . We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls, to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can update or correct your profile information from within the app at any time.
  • You can disable location or camera permissions from your device settings.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us at the email below — we will delete your personal data except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

CarPool is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
